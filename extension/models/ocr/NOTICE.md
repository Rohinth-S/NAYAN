# Local credential OCR attribution

The extension packages the English `eng.traineddata` file from the
[`tessdata_fast`](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast) project at a
pinned revision. Tesseract.js and tesseract.js-core are distributed under the
Apache License 2.0. The OCR asset is used locally for credential geometry
only; OCR text is not retained or sent to the reasoning service.
