⚠️ Clarification for Judges: 9 Masked Regions — NOT 9 Separate Documents

The previous raw list printed [AADHAAR_CARD_1] through [AADHAAR_CARD_6], which misleadingly made it look like 6 separate cards existed. In reality, there is only ONE physical Aadhaar card and ONE PAN card on the page. Because a single identity card contains multiple distinct sensitive fields (photo portrait, 12-digit UID, name, date of birth, mobile number, and address QR), the local privacy engine creates an individual mask for each zone.
